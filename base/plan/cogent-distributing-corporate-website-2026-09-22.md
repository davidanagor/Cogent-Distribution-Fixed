# Cogent Distributing Corporate Website

## Goal
Build a production-quality, multi-page corporate website for Cogent Distributing LLC that positions the company as the professional bridge between U.S. supply and West African demand. The site will use the supplied navy, royal blue, sky blue, white, charcoal, steel gray, and restrained red brand system without copying the reference website.

## Visual Direction
- Create a cinematic editorial system with full-width logistics photography, deep navy image treatments, strong sans-serif typography, thin red directional details, oversized indexes, clean grid lines, and generous off-white space.
- Use Manrope for display and body typography, with tight authoritative headings and highly readable supporting copy.
- Generate a cohesive set of realistic logistics imagery covering ports, containers, freight handling, vehicles, warehousing, and trade infrastructure; avoid generic office imagery and repetitive container shots.
- Carry the recurring `NORTH AMERICA ↔ WEST AFRICA` concept through route graphics, section dividers, process diagrams, calls to action, and the footer without claiming unverified routes.
- Add restrained motion: navbar transformation, image masks, section reveals, route-line movement, button arrows, and responsive mobile navigation, all respecting reduced-motion settings.

## Shared Site System
- Build a sticky transparent-to-navy navigation with active-route styling, a services dropdown, mobile slide-out navigation, and working links to every page.
- Build reusable page heroes, section headers, editorial image blocks, service cards, process timelines, route visualization, breadcrumbs, calls to action, contact form, legal-page layout, and global footer.
- Store services, customer groups, process stages, contact details, and future case-study entries in structured data so content can expand without duplicating page code.
- Apply the supplied semantic color system globally and keep all page styling token-based.

## Pages
- Home: cinematic hero, Cogent introduction, six-service preview, transatlantic connection visual, five-stage process, customer groups, and closing quote CTA.
- About: company purpose, exact supplied mission and vision, core values, and U.S. representation.
- Services overview: editorial service grid and complete source-to-distribution diagram.
- Service detail pages: Procurement, Automobile Logistics, Shipping & Freight, Warehousing & Packing, and Distribution & Logistics, each with its requested content, process, imagery, and quote CTA.
- Industries: five audience-specific sections using only the supplied customer groups and verified service language.
- Logistics: detailed five-stage animated process with service relationships and responsive timeline.
- Case Studies: polished empty-state architecture for future verified stories, without fabricated projects.
- Contact: verified company information and a validated quote form with file selection and an honest integration-ready state; no fake transmission.
- Privacy and Terms: clearly labeled, structured draft policy pages that do not imply legal review.

## Routing, SEO, and Quality
- Create every requested route as a real TanStack page with working navigation, browser history, breadcrumbs, service links, footer links, and `/contact` quote CTAs.
- Add unique page titles, descriptions, Open Graph text, Twitter card metadata, semantic heading structure, and meaningful image alt text to every route.
- Ensure desktop, tablet, and mobile compositions are intentionally designed with no overflow, usable touch targets, responsive imagery, and an appropriate mobile quote action.
- Verify every route, dropdown/menu interaction, form validation and completion state, back/forward navigation, visual loading, mobile layout, and browser console before completion.

## Content Guardrails
- Use only the company facts, service descriptions, address, phone numbers, email, mission, vision, and audiences supplied in the brief.
- Do not invent routes, ports, transit times, partnerships, certifications, capacity, tracking, customs services, leadership, history, metrics, clients, testimonials, case studies, legal policies, or business hours.
- Keep the quote form frontend-only until a real backend or email destination is connected, and state that clearly in the interface.
